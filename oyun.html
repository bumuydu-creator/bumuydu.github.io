Chatforce: Build & Share Viral Games with AI

Chatforce is an AI-powered game studio. Four AI agents turn your ideas into playable, shareable games in minutes. No coding required.

Your AI Game Studio

Director: Designs the game concept, mechanics, and level structure based on your description.

Coder: Writes the game code, handles logic, physics, and interactivity.

Artist: Generates sprites, backgrounds, and visual assets in your chosen art style.

Musician: Composes music and creates sound effects for your game.

How It Works

1. Describe your game idea in plain language. 2. Your AI agents build it collaboratively. 3. Play, test, and iterate with feedback. 4. Share with one click so anyone can play in their browser.

Who Is Chatforce For?

Anyone with a game idea: beginners with no coding experience, indie creators who want to prototype fast, students and educators building interactive projects, content creators making games for their audience, and experienced developers accelerating early production.

Full Creative Control

You have complete creative control: choose art styles, upload reference images, choose a stylized font, and paint over your concept art to show your agents what you want changed. Your agents will follow all your creative requirements. The result is a more cohesive, higher-quality game than any single-model tool can produce.
